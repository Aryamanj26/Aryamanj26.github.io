CFAR for Indoor mmWave Human Sensing

Developing application-specific CFAR methods for indoor human activity sensing that exploit target-relevant structure in range–Doppler maps to improve detection of extended human returns while suppressing multipath-induced ghost points. A TI AWR6843ISK and DCA1000 provide raw radar captures, while a RealSense D455 supplies reference regions of interest for measuring missed detections and false alarms.
